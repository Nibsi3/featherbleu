Access Control for Garden Route Businesses

A practical guide to keypads, tags, biometrics, intercoms and mobile access for Garden Route offices, workshops and small businesses.

Featherbleu Industries · Updated 30 September 2026 · Garden Route

Business access control answers three questions: who may enter, where may they go and when is access allowed? A suitable system makes those decisions easier to manage without slowing down normal work.

Map people and doors before choosing hardware

List staff, managers, cleaners, contractors, visitors and delivery teams. Then identify which entrances or rooms each group needs. This simple access map prevents overcomplicated systems and shows where an ordinary lock is sufficient.

PIN keypads

Keypads are cost-effective and familiar. Use individual codes rather than one shared PIN, change compromised codes immediately and avoid placing the keypad where someone can easily observe entries.

Cards, tags and mobile credentials

Credentials can be issued and removed without changing a physical lock. They work well for teams that change over time and can create a useful access history. Lost credentials should be cancelled promptly.

Biometric access

Biometrics can suit higher-control areas or sites where shared credentials are a concern. Consider enrolment, privacy, backup access and how the system will work for visitors or people whose fingerprint is difficult to read.

Intercom and visitor management

Audio or video intercoms allow reception or a responsible staff member to verify a visitor before opening. Clear delivery and visitor procedures are just as important as the device itself.

Integrate only where it adds value

Access control can work with gates, doors, CCTV and alarms, but every integration should have a clear purpose. Keep emergency release, power backup and manual procedures simple and documented.

Review access regularly

Remove former employees, audit active credentials and test backup power. A short quarterly review keeps the system aligned with the team and avoids a long list of forgotten users.
