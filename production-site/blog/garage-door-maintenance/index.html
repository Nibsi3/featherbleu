Garage Door Maintenance: A Complete Guide

Keep your garage door safe, quiet and reliable with practical monthly, seasonal and annual maintenance checks for Garden Route homes.

Featherbleu Industries · Updated 30 September 2026 · Garden Route

A garage door is heavy, used frequently and exposed to coastal conditions. Regular observation and timely servicing help it stay safe, balanced and dependable.

Monthly checks

Watch the door complete a full opening and closing cycle. It should move smoothly without shuddering, scraping or leaning. Listen for new grinding or straining sounds. Check that the remote responds consistently and that the door closes fully without bouncing back.

Keep tracks and the opening clear

Remove stones, leaves and other debris from the tracks and threshold. Do not apply random grease to tracks or rollers: some components require a specific lubricant while others should remain clean and dry. If the door binds, stop using it until the cause is identified.

Test the safety features

Photoelectric beams should be clean, aligned and free from obstructions. Confirm that the door stops or reverses when a safety device is triggered. Never bypass a safety beam simply to keep the door operating.

Inspect visible hardware

Look for loose fasteners, worn cables, damaged hinges, bent tracks and corrosion. Springs and cables store significant energy and should only be adjusted or replaced by a trained technician. Keep hands and tools away from moving parts.

Plan for coastal weather and outages

Salt, moisture and wind can accelerate wear on Garden Route properties. Clean exposed surfaces, address corrosion early and ask whether the current finish suits the site. Test the backup battery so the door can still operate when grid power is unavailable.

When to call a professional

Arrange a service if the door becomes unusually heavy, moves unevenly, makes repeated loud noises, reverses without an obvious obstruction or shows damage to springs, cables or tracks. Early attention usually prevents more expensive damage.
