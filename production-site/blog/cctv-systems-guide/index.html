CCTV Systems: What You Need to Know

Understand CCTV camera types, image quality, night vision, recording, remote viewing and placement before installing a surveillance system.

Featherbleu Industries · Updated 30 September 2026 · Garden Route

A useful CCTV system captures the right detail, stores it reliably and makes important footage easy to find. Camera count matters less than careful placement and a clear purpose for every view.

Choose cameras around the scene

Entrances and driveways need enough detail to recognise people or vehicles. Wider areas may need overview coverage as well as a tighter identification view. Indoor, outdoor, low-light and coastal locations each have different requirements, so one camera model rarely suits every position.

Understand recording options

A local recorder keeps footage at the property, while cloud services can add off-site access or storage. Consider retention time, image quality, available bandwidth and what happens during an internet or power interruption. The system should make exporting an incident straightforward.

Plan night performance

Infrared illumination can provide clear monochrome footage, while suitable lighting or colour night technology may preserve useful colour detail. Avoid pointing cameras directly at bright lights or reflective surfaces, which can hide the subject you need to see.

Use remote alerts carefully

Motion alerts are valuable when they identify meaningful activity. Poorly configured alerts become noise and are eventually ignored. Define zones, schedules and detection settings around actual risks, then test them at different times of day.

Protect the system itself

Use unique passwords, keep software current and restrict who can view recordings. Place recorders and network equipment where they are not easy to remove. Battery backup can keep cameras and connectivity operating through short outages.

Professional placement makes the difference

Before drilling or cabling, walk the site and agree on the question each camera must answer. Check the final live view, night view, recording quality and remote access with the installer before the job is signed off.
