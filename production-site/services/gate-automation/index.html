Repairs & installations

Find the right next step for your gate.

When a gate motor needs attention

A gate that stops midway, reverses unexpectedly, moves slowly or ignores a remote needs fault-finding before parts are replaced. The cause may involve power, a battery, safety beams, the remote, the motor or the gate’s mechanical movement.

Keep the opening clear and note any display message or unusual sound. Do not bypass safety devices or force a heavy gate. Send a photo of the motor label and describe when the problem occurs.

Choosing sliding or swing automation

The motor must suit the gate’s weight, travel and frequency of use. A sliding gate also needs a sound track and smooth movement; a swing gate needs suitable hinges, opening space and mounting points.

For a new installation, discuss power supply, battery backup, safe closing and how visitors will enter. Remotes, intercoms and connected control should fit the way the property is used.

What affects a gate automation quote?

Gate size and condition, the motor required, daily use, cable runs, electrical supply and civil or mechanical work all affect the cost. Safety beams, intercoms, additional remotes, backup power and suitable solar options can also change the scope.

A photo helps start the conversation, but the owners may need a site assessment to confirm the work and provide a useful quote. Ask what is included and which equipment and warranty terms apply to your installation.

Make your enquiry easier to assess

Common questions

Before you book a gate assessment.

Do I need a new motor if my gate will not open?

Not necessarily. A power, battery, remote, safety-device or mechanical fault can look like a motor failure. Explain the symptoms so the owners can discuss diagnosis before recommending replacement.

Can an existing gate be automated?

That depends on its structure, condition and movement, along with the available power and space. Share photos of the whole gate and its track or hinges; a site assessment can establish what needs attention first.
