01

When direct contact matters

If you are coordinating a gate, garage door and access changes, it helps to know who is making the installation decisions. With Featherbleu, you can describe the property and discuss the work directly with Werner or Joe.

02

Different team models can work well

A provider with separate scheduling and installation teams may suit a project that needs several crews or a particular booking window. Ask how information passes between the people quoting and the people doing the work.

03

The Featherbleu fit

Featherbleu suits customers who value owner involvement and a named contact. Confirm availability early and agree the work, access arrangements and any changes before the installation proceeds.

Before you decide

Three questions worth asking.

Use the same questions with every provider so the answers are easier to compare.

  1. Who will actually attend my property?
  2. Who approves changes to the scope and price?
  3. Who should I contact if I need help after the job?
