01

Repair is a question worth asking

A failed remote or a power-related problem does not tell you, on its own, that the entire motor needs replacing. Describe the fault and request an assessment. The condition of the equipment and the findings should guide the recommendation.

02

Replacement can be the sensible choice

A new installation may be appropriate when the existing system cannot meet your requirements or the work needed is extensive. Ask the provider to explain the reason and compare the scope of repair and replacement where both are viable.

03

The Featherbleu fit

Because Featherbleu offers installation, fault-finding and servicing, you can discuss more than one route with the owners. Agree the assessment cost, proposed work and follow-up arrangements before proceeding.

Before you decide

Three questions worth asking.

Use the same questions with every provider so the answers are easier to compare.

  1. What fault was identified, and what alternatives were considered?
  2. What is included in the total price and what could be extra?
  3. What maintenance and written warranty terms apply?
