01

Verify the person as well as the business

CENTURION certifies individuals, with annual renewal required. Ask which certified installer will carry out your job and confirm their current details before booking.

02

Match experience to the actual job

A driveway gate, a garage door and a shared entrance have different requirements. Explain the system you have, its condition and how it is used. Ask for examples relevant to your project rather than relying only on a brand name.

03

Why choose Featherbleu?

You can start with a public MyInstaller profile, view real project examples, and speak directly with the owners about your property. That gives you practical evidence to use when comparing quotes.

Before you decide

Three questions worth asking.

Use the same questions with every provider so the answers are easier to compare.

  1. Who is the certified installer assigned to my property?
  2. Can I check the current listing and relevant project examples?
  3. Which equipment, labour and support are included in the written quote?
