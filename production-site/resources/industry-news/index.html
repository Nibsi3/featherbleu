Connected security

One view of the property

Cameras, access and automation increasingly work together, reducing the number of separate apps owners need to manage.

Smarter alerts

Fewer, more useful notifications

Improved detection can distinguish between ordinary movement and events that deserve attention.

Backup power

Resilience is now part of the design

Battery and solar options are being considered at the start of installations, rather than as later additions.

Mobile access

The phone becomes a credential

Secure mobile access is making it easier to grant temporary or controlled entry without sharing physical keys.

Privacy

Better control of recordings

Property owners are paying closer attention to who can view footage, how long it is stored and where it is kept.

Interoperability

Choose systems that can grow

Open, compatible products reduce the risk of replacing an entire installation when one component changes.
