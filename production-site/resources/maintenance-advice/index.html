M
Garage doors

Listen for changes

New scraping, shuddering or straining sounds are signs to stop and inspect before the problem worsens.

Gate motors

Keep the track clear

Remove stones, soil and plant growth. Check that the gate travels freely before blaming the motor.

CCTV

Clean and review the view

Dust, salt and spiderwebs reduce image quality. Confirm that plants or new structures have not blocked the view.

Backup power

Test batteries regularly

Batteries deteriorate quietly. Test performance before the next power outage exposes the problem.

Access control

Review active users

Remove old credentials, update shared codes and keep an accurate record of who should have access.

Professional service

Do not force moving parts

If a heavy gate or door binds, stop using it and arrange a service to avoid injury or further damage.
